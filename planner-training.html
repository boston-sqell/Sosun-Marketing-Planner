Planner Workflow

Interactive training guide for managing tasks across multiple views in the new Planner Tab.

How a Task Moves

Every task in the system follows a standard lifecycle. While you can view tasks in different ways, the underlying workflow remains consistent.

1. Creation

Tasks begin their life in the "To Do" status. You can create them from the List View by clicking "+ Add task" under the To Do group.

2. Execution

When you start work, move the task to "In Progress". If using the Board View, simply drag the card to the next column.

3. Completion

Once work is finished, move it to "Review" for QA/Approval, and finally to "Done" when the deliverable is accepted.

List View

Groups tasks by status in a collapsible list format. Best for backlog grooming, editing nested subtasks, and getting a detailed overview of all properties.

Workload Dashboard

Shows capacity across the team. View completion rings and unassigned tasks. Drag tasks between team members to instantly rebalance workloads.

Pro Tips & AI Automation

Take your productivity to the next level with custom views and automated teammates.

🔍 Saved Views

Frequently filtering for specific tasks? (e.g., "High Priority assigned to me"). Apply your filters and hit "Save as view". It will pin as a new tab for instant access later.

🔗 Dependency Management

In the Timeline view, connect tasks that rely on each other. If a blocker task is delayed, the system can automatically highlight or shift dependent tasks.

🤖 The Campaign Agent

Meet your AI teammate! The system includes a virtual user (`agent:campaign`). Assign specific tasks to the agent, and it will work in the background to draft briefs, summarize research, or generate ideas. The agent will post its work as a comment and transition the task when finished. Look for the live "Prioritizing..." badge when it's actively working.
